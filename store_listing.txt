FULL DESCRIPTION:
FixEase is your personal AI mechanic, available 24/7. Diagnose your car's health using symptoms, photos, or real-time video — powered by Google's Gemini AI.

★ SMART DIAGNOSTICS
• Urgent Mode — Select symptoms, set severity, get instant AI analysis
• Photo Mode — Snap up to 8 photos of the issue; AI detects damage, leaks, wear
• Gemini Live (Beta) — Point your camera and chat with AI in real-time

★ HEALTH SCORING
• Overall health score (0–10) with confidence rating
• Component breakdown: Engine, Brakes, Transmission, Electrical, Suspension
• Visual radar chart for at-a-glance health fingerprint
• Track health over time with saved reports

★ FULL REPORTS
• AI summary with possible causes and actionable recommendations
• Urgency alerts for critical issues
• Report history with search, view, and delete
• Export-ready detailed breakdowns
